# Privacy Policy – Viewport Search

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

Viewport Search does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or user data.

- **Page content:** When you start a search, the extension reads the text of the current page in your browser only to find and highlight matches. That text never leaves your device, and the page content is not modified.
- **Search terms:** Your search term is kept in memory only while the page is open. It is not saved or sent anywhere.
- **Settings:** The selected match mode (substring, prefix, or whole word) is saved in the extension's local browser storage so the popup remembers it. It stays on your device.
- **Network:** The extension makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
- **Third parties:** No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party services are used.
